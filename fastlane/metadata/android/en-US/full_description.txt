Kira HRT is a private record book for hormone replacement therapy: what you took, when, and what your blood tests say.

Everything is stored on the device first. An account is optional — without one the app is a complete offline record book. With one, records sync between your devices through a server you can run yourself, and every record is sealed with a key derived from your password before it leaves the phone.

WHAT IT DOES
• Log doses with reusable one-tap templates, per route and ester
• Model your estradiol curve from the doses you actually logged, with four selectable calibration methods
• Record lab results and compare measured values against the modelled curve
• Read a lab report from a photo — OCR runs on the device, the image never leaves it
• Reminders for scheduled doses, plus an overview of what is due and what is overdue
• Export and import (JSON/CSV), including profiles from other trackers
• Light and dark themes, seven languages, phone and tablet layouts

PRIVACY
No ads. No analytics. No third-party trackers. The optional sync server stores records encrypted with a per-account key, and holds no key material of its own.

OPEN SOURCE
MIT licensed. The app and the optional server are in the same repository, so you can audit exactly what leaves the device — or run the server yourself. This build contains no Firebase Cloud Messaging and does not check for or install updates; updates come from F-Droid.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
This is a personal record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It does not recommend doses. Those decisions belong to you and your prescriber.
