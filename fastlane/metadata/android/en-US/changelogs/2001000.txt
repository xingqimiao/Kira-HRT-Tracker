2.1.0

- Security: fixed key rotation breaking permanently after a password change; accounts already affected repair themselves on the next change.
- Security: medication plans are now stored per account, so switching accounts no longer inherits the previous user's schedule or reminders.
- Security: content security policy for web and app; share links no longer fall back to plaintext; update packages are verified by SHA-256.
- New: "sign out and clear this device".
- Fixed: the add-dose time picker steps minutes by one and accepts typed digits, like the other pickers.
- Fixed: closing the new-plan panel with the system back gesture takes one swipe.
