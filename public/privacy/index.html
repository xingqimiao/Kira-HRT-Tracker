Privacy Policy — Kira Tracker

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Kira Tracker is a medication and blood-test tracker for people on hormone replacement therapy. It is operated by KiraMyao Equal ("we"). This page explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how to have it changed or deleted.

1. What we store

  • Account data. The username you choose, a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself), and — if you sign in with X or Google — the identifier that provider gives us for your account.
  • Health data you enter. Doses, medication and preparation names, blood-test results, and any notes you add.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs produced by handling a request (IP address, time, user agent) and the encrypted session credential held by your browser.

2. Data received from Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", we request the openid scope only. Google returns a single opaque account identifier (the sub claim) inside a signed ID token. We do not request, and do not receive, your Google name, email address, profile picture, contacts, Gmail, Drive or Calendar content, or any other Google user data.

We use that identifier for one purpose only: to recognise that a returning sign-in belongs to the same Kira Tracker account. It is stored alongside your account record in our database. We do not use it to build a profile, to advertise, to score you, or for any purpose other than signing you in.

Limited Use. Kira Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data received from X

When you sign in with X, we receive your numeric X user ID, your username, display name and avatar URL, and we store them against your account so the app can show who is signed in. We never see or store your X password, and we do not read your posts, direct messages, followers or any other X content.

4. How your records are protected

Your health records are encrypted before they are written to disk, using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is held separately from the database. A stolen database dump is therefore not readable on its own. Transport to and from the app is over HTTPS.

This is server-side encryption, not end-to-end encryption: the server can decrypt your records in order to serve them back to you. We do not claim otherwise.

5. Who processes your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We use a small number of infrastructure providers, who process only what is needed to run the service: Cloudflare (DNS, CDN and abuse protection) and Oracle Cloud (server hosting, Singapore region). Cloudflare Turnstile is used on the registration form to block automated signups. If you sign in with Google or X, that provider processes the sign-in request under its own privacy policy.

Your health records are never sent to Google, X, or any analytics or advertising service. We run no third-party analytics or tracking scripts in the app.

If you connect an AI assistant. The app offers an access token for connecting an assistant to your records over MCP. That token carries no decryption key, so on its own it cannot open your account — but while you are signed in it can read your records, and each read keeps the session alive. Anything the assistant reads leaves this system and is then governed by that provider's own privacy policy, not by this one. Revoke the token in Settings when you stop using it.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app; deleting it removes your account, your sign-in links and your records from our database. You can also email us to request a copy of your data or its deletion.

Two limits we would rather state than leave you to discover. Deletion is immediate and complete in the live database, but it cannot reach a backup that has already been taken. Database backups are retained on a rolling basis and age out, so a deleted record can persist inside an older backup until that backup expires; backups exist only for disaster recovery and are not used to serve or analyse your data. And individual records you delete in the app are deleted outright — there is no hidden tombstone holding the content.

7. Your rights and contact

You may ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will respond within 30 days. Contact: report@kiramyao.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we will update the date at the top of this page and tell you in the app.

隐私说明 — Kira Tracker

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 19 日

Kira Tracker 是由 KiraMyao Equal 运营的激素替代治疗（HRT）用药与化验记录工具。本页说明我们收集哪些信息、为什么收集、由谁处理、保存多久，以及如何修改或删除。

1. 我们保存什么

  • 账号信息：你设置的账号名、密码的加盐哈希（我们不保存密码本身）；若使用 X 或 Google 登录，则保存该平台提供的账号标识。
  • 你录入的健康数据：用药记录、药物与制剂名称、化验结果，以及你填写的备注。
  • 技术信息：处理请求时必然产生的服务器日志（IP 地址、时间、浏览器标识），以及浏览器中保存的加密会话凭据。

2. 来自 Google 的数据

使用「使用 Google 登录」时，我们只申请 openid 权限。Google 返回一个不可逆的账号标识（ID token 中的 sub）。我们不会申请、也不会获得你的 Google 姓名、邮箱地址、头像，以及通讯录、Gmail、Drive、日历内容或任何其他 Google 用户数据。

该标识只用于一件事：识别某次登录属于同一个 Kira Tracker 账号。它保存在我们的账号记录中，不用于画像、广告、评分，也不用于登录以外的任何目的。

有限使用：Kira Tracker 对 Google API 所获信息的使用遵循 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。

3. 来自 X 的数据

使用 X 登录时，我们会获得并保存你的 X 数字用户 ID、用户名、显示名称与头像地址，用于在应用内显示当前登录的人。我们不会接触或保存你的 X 密码，也不会读取你的推文、私信、关注关系或任何其他 X 内容。

4. 你的记录如何被保护

健康记录在写入磁盘前使用 AES-256-GCM 加密，密钥与数据库分开保存。因此，单独拖走数据库的文件无法读取其中的内容。应用与服务端之间的传输使用 HTTPS。

这是服务端加密，不是端到端加密：服务器为了把记录发回给你，必须能够解密。我们不做相反的宣称。

5. 谁会处理你的数据

我们不出售数据，也不与广告商或数据中间商共享。我们只使用运行服务所必需的基础设施服务商：Cloudflare（DNS、CDN 与防护）与 Oracle Cloud（服务器托管，新加坡区域）。注册表单使用 Cloudflare Turnstile 拦截自动化注册。若你使用 Google 或 X 登录，该平台会按其自身隐私政策处理这次登录请求。

你的健康记录不会被发送给 Google、X，也不会进入任何统计或广告服务。应用中不包含第三方统计脚本。

如果你连接了 AI 助手。应用提供访问令牌，可通过 MCP 让助手读取你的记录。该令牌不携带解密密钥，本身无法解锁你的账号；但在你登录期间它可以读取记录，且每次读取都会延长会话。助手读到的内容会离开本系统，此后受该服务商的隐私政策约束，而不是本说明。不再使用时请在「设置」中吊销令牌。

6. 保存期限与删除

账号存在期间我们会保存这些数据。你可以随时在应用内的「设置」中删除账号；删除后，账号、登录绑定关系与记录会从数据库中移除。你也可以通过邮件请求导出或删除数据。

有两个限制我们宁愿直接说明，而不是让你自己发现。删除在实时数据库中立即且彻底，但无法触及已经生成的备份。数据库备份按滚动周期保留并会过期，因此被删除的记录可能在一份较旧的备份里继续存在，直到该备份过期；备份仅用于灾难恢复，不会被用来提供或分析你的数据。另外，你在应用内删除的单条记录是直接删除的——没有隐藏的墓碑保留其内容。

7. 你的权利与联系方式

你可以要求获取数据副本、更正数据或删除数据，我们会在 30 天内回复。联系方式： report@kiramyao.com。

8. 变更

若本说明的变更会影响你的数据处理方式，我们会更新页首日期，并在应用内告知。

KiraMyao Equal 整体项目（网站、问卷、投稿、故事）的完整隐私与数据处理说明见 kiramyao.com/privacy。两者如有冲突，以本页关于 Kira Tracker 的部分为准。
