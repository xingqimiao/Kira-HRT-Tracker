Kira HRT Tracker

An HRT log. Write down every dose, and see a curve that looks like yours.

Open the web app Download for Android GitHub

The more you log,
the better it knows you.

Everyone metabolises at their own pace. Enter a blood test result and the curve moves toward your real levels — and it knows to set aside the occasional reading that clearly missed.

Log it the way
you took it.

Oral, sublingual, gel, injection, patch. How long you held it under your tongue, where the gel went, when you peeled the patch off — the small things that change absorption are all one tap away.

The doses you take
every day, one tap.

Save your regular doses as one-tap templates. No retyping each time, and with a schedule reminder set, checking in is a light press when the time comes.

The numbers on the report,
finally readable.

Put your blood tests side by side and see how far the estimate drifts from what was measured. When it is time to re-check liver function or hormone levels, it reminds you.

Photograph the report.
The photo stays on your phone.

A local neural network reads the hormone values straight out of the image and fills the form in for you. The photo never leaves your device and is never uploaded.

Hundreds of times,
kept clearly.

Walk back through every day of dosing on a timeline. Whatever you used before — Oyama, Transmtf or Featherline — your old records import straight in.

Talk to your AI assistant
directly.

Once an assistant is connected there is no file to export. Tell it what you took today, or ask how your levels have been moving, and it will log it and walk through it with you.

“Just applied 2.6 mg of estradiol gel — log it and check my level?”
“Logged 2.60 mg of gel for you! The estimated level is 133.5 pg/mL, inside your target range.”

Every day it has
been with you, kept locked.

Everything works offline without an account. Turn on cloud sync and your data is encrypted by both your password and a hardware security module — even a read-only link you share leaves no plaintext on the server.
