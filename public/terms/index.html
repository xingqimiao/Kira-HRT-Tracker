3. What the Service is

The Service lets you record hormone replacement therapy doses and blood test results, and estimates hormone levels over time from those records using a published pharmacokinetic model.

The output of that model is an estimate. Section 4 explains what that means and what you may not use it for — please read it before relying on any number the Service shows you.

4. Medical disclaimer

The Service is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice.

Every level the Service displays is the output of a pharmacokinetic model — an estimate, not a measurement. It is calculated from a population model, published parameters, and the doses you have logged. It has not been reviewed by a clinician, and it is not a laboratory result.

Specifically, and without limitation:

  • Estimates can be wrong, in either direction. A population model describes an average. Real clearance, absorption and bioavailability vary substantially between individuals, and the Service cannot know yours unless you supply blood test results to calibrate against.
  • Do not use the Service to decide a dose, to change a dose, or to decide whether to skip one. Decisions about medication are between you and your prescriber.
  • Do not use it to check whether a dose has taken effect, or to detect a problem. Only a blood test can measure your levels.
  • Do not use it as a diagnosis or as a treatment recommendation. It offers neither.
  • Do not use it in place of blood tests or clinical care. If your prescriber has asked for monitoring, keep those appointments.
  • Do not rely on it if you feel unwell. Seek medical attention.
  • Do not use the Service in a medical emergency. Contact emergency services.

You are responsible for how you use the output. If anything the Service displays conflicts with what a clinician tells you, follow the clinician.
