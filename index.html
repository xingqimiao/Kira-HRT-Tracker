Kira HRT - HRT 激素治疗记录与用药追踪

Kira HRT is an agent-friendly HRT tracker for medications, lab results, treatment history, and pharmacokinetic hormone-level estimates.

Kira HRT 是面向 AI Agent 的 HRT 记录工具，用于记录用药、化验结果和治疗历程， 并提供基于药代动力学模型的激素水平估算。

功能 / Features

  • 记录用药：药物、制剂、剂量与时间 / Log doses: drug, preparation, amount and time
  • 记录化验结果并跟踪长期趋势 / Log blood-test results and follow long-term trends
  • 根据剂量与时间推算激素水平的估算曲线 / Estimate hormone-level curves from your doses and timing
  • 记录以 AES-256-GCM 加密保存 / Records are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM
  • 使用账号名与密码、X 或 Google 登录 / Sign in with a username and password, X, or Google

AI 助手友好 / Agent-friendly

Kira HRT Tracker 提供 MCP 接口，AI 助手可以直接读取并写入你的记录，不需要你手动导出文件。

Kira HRT Tracker speaks MCP, so an AI assistant can read and write your record directly — no manual export. The endpoint, the transport, and every tool are documented in server/MCP.md.

本工具不是医疗器械，也不提供医疗建议；估算曲线不是实测值，用药决定请遵循处方医师的意见。

Kira HRT Tracker is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
